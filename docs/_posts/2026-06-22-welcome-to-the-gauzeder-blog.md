This blog is the place where the Gauzeder team shares engineering notes, architecture decisions, and project updates alongside the structured documentation.

## What you'll find here

- **Engineering notes** — deep-dives into technical decisions, trade-offs, and lessons learned during development.
- **Architecture decisions** — commentary on the ADRs and how we applied DDD, Hexagonal Architecture, and event-driven patterns.
- **Project updates** — announcements about new features, releases, and changes to the project roadmap.

## How posts are organised

Posts are grouped by **category** (broad topic area) and **tag** (specific concept). Use the chips on this page and on each post to browse by either dimension.

## Authoring a new post

Add a file to `docs/_posts/` following the `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md` naming convention with the front-matter below:

```yaml
---
title: "Your Post Title"
author: "Your Name"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
categories: [category-name]
tags: [tag1, tag2]
---
```

The post will automatically appear in the blog listing, in the relevant tag and category archive pages, and linked from the site header.
